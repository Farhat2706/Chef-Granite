Avocado Toast Variations
================================
Servings: 2 | Prep: 10 min | Cook: 5 min | Total: 15 min
Cuisine: Modern American | Category: Breakfast/Brunch

BASE RECIPE:
- 2 slices sourdough or whole grain bread
- 2 ripe avocados
- 1 lemon, juiced
- Salt and black pepper
- Red pepper flakes

INSTRUCTIONS (Base):
1. Toast the bread to your desired crispness.
2. Cut avocados in half, remove pit, and scoop flesh into a bowl.
3. Mash with a fork. Add lemon juice, salt, and pepper to taste.
4. Spread generously on toasted bread.
5. Top with red pepper flakes and any additional toppings.

VARIATION 1 - The Classic:
- Toppings: Everything bagel seasoning, microgreens, lemon zest

VARIATION 2 - Mediterranean:
- Toppings: Crumbled feta cheese, halved cherry tomatoes, kalamata olives, fresh oregano

VARIATION 3 - Protein Power:
- Toppings: 2 poached eggs, everything bagel seasoning, smoked salmon

VARIATION 4 - Spicy Southwest:
- Toppings: Pico de gallo, jalapeño slices, cotija cheese, lime zest, cilantro

VARIATION 5 - Caprese Style:
- Toppings: Fresh mozzarella, heirloom tomato slices, fresh basil, balsamic glaze

DIETARY INFO (Base):
- Vegan, Dairy-Free, Gluten-Free (with GF bread)
- Calories per serving: ~320 kcal
- Fat: 22g | Carbs: 28g | Protein: 6g | Fiber: 10g

TIPS:
- Use ripe avocados — they should give slightly when pressed
- Lemon juice prevents browning and brightens flavor
- Sourdough provides the best crunch and flavor contrast

NUTRITIONAL HIGHLIGHTS:
- Avocados are rich in heart-healthy monounsaturated fats
- High in potassium, fiber, and vitamins K, E, C, and B-6
