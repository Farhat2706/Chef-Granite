Pasta Primavera
================================
Servings: 4 | Prep: 15 min | Cook: 20 min | Total: 35 min
Cuisine: Italian | Category: Main Course

INGREDIENTS:
- 400g penne or spaghetti pasta
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 zucchini, sliced
- 1 yellow bell pepper, diced
- 1 red bell pepper, diced
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 cup broccoli florets
- 1/2 cup frozen peas
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- Fresh basil leaves
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- Red pepper flakes (optional)

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Cook pasta in heavily salted boiling water according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1 cup pasta water before draining.
2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add garlic and sauté for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add broccoli and bell peppers. Cook for 4-5 minutes until slightly tender.
4. Add zucchini and cherry tomatoes. Cook for another 3 minutes.
5. Stir in frozen peas and heavy cream. Simmer for 2 minutes.
6. Add drained pasta to the skillet. Toss to combine, adding pasta water as needed to loosen the sauce.
7. Remove from heat, stir in Parmesan cheese and fresh basil.
8. Season with salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes.

DIETARY INFO:
- Contains: Gluten, Dairy
- Calories per serving: ~480 kcal
- Fat: 18g | Carbs: 65g | Protein: 16g | Fiber: 6g

SUBSTITUTIONS:
- Vegan version: Omit cream and Parmesan, use coconut cream and nutritional yeast
- Gluten-free: Use gluten-free pasta
- Lower calorie: Replace heavy cream with low-fat milk + 1 tsp cornstarch
- Add protein: Toss in grilled chicken, shrimp, or white beans

TIPS:
- Don't overcook the vegetables — they should still have a slight crunch
- Pasta water is starchy and helps the sauce cling to the pasta
- Any seasonal vegetables work great in this recipe
