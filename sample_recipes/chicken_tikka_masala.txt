Chicken Tikka Masala
================================
Servings: 6 | Prep: 30 min | Cook: 40 min | Total: 70 min (plus 2 hrs marinating)
Cuisine: Indian | Category: Main Course

INGREDIENTS:

For the Marinade:
- 1.5 lbs (700g) boneless chicken thighs, cut into 2-inch pieces
- 1 cup plain whole-milk yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp salt

For the Masala Sauce:
- 3 tbsp butter or ghee
- 1 large onion, finely diced
- 5 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- 2 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp cayenne pepper (adjust to taste)
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Salt to taste
- Fresh cilantro for garnish

INSTRUCTIONS:
1. Combine all marinade ingredients with chicken. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours (overnight is best).
2. Preheat broiler to high. Thread chicken onto skewers or spread on a baking sheet.
3. Broil chicken for 8-10 minutes, turning once, until charred at edges. Set aside.
4. In a large pot, melt butter over medium heat. Add onion and cook until golden, about 10 minutes.
5. Add garlic and ginger, cook for 2 minutes.
6. Add cumin, coriander, paprika, garam masala, and cayenne. Stir for 1 minute until fragrant.
7. Pour in crushed tomatoes. Simmer for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
8. Use an immersion blender to puree the sauce until smooth (optional for smoother texture).
9. Stir in heavy cream and broiled chicken. Simmer for 10 more minutes.
10. Season with salt and serve over basmati rice, garnished with cilantro.

DIETARY INFO:
- Contains: Dairy
- Calories per serving: ~420 kcal
- Fat: 26g | Carbs: 12g | Protein: 38g

SUBSTITUTIONS:
- Dairy-free: Use coconut yogurt for marinade, coconut cream instead of heavy cream
- Vegan: Replace chicken with chickpeas and cauliflower, use coconut yogurt and coconut cream
- Low-fat: Use Greek yogurt instead of heavy cream (add at the end off heat)

TIPS:
- The longer you marinate the chicken, the more flavorful and tender it will be
- Charring the chicken under the broiler adds authentic tandoor flavor
- This dish tastes even better the next day as flavors meld

SERVING SUGGESTIONS:
- Basmati rice or naan bread
- Cucumber raita (yogurt with cucumber and mint)
- Mango chutney
